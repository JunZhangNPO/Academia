### Russell Sage Foundation (RSF)
* **Mission:** Founded in 1907 in NYC, RSF is dedicated to empirical social science research aimed at improving social and living conditions in the U.S.
* **Methodological Emphasis:** Highly prioritizes quantitative research, causal inference (e.g., DiD, RCTs, RDD, Instrumental Variables), structural econometrics, and large-scale data analysis.

---

### Priority Research Areas
* **Social, Political, and Economic Inequality (SPEI):** Wealth/income mobility, safety net programs, public policy impacts, and education disparities.
* **Future of Work (FOW):** Structural changes in the labor market, low-wage work, worker voice/governance, technology/automation, and job quality.
* **Behavioral Science and Decision Making in Context (BSDMC):** Applying behavioral economics and psychology to social/institutional decision-making.
* **Race, Ethnicity, and Immigration (REI):** Immigration integration, institutional discrimination, systemic disparities, and identity dynamics.
* **Special Initiatives:** Margaret Olivia Sage (MOS) Grants (Democracy, AI, Infrastructure) and Causal Research on the Criminal Justice System.

### RSF Research Grant Portfolio

| Grant Program | Target Audience | Max Funding | Key Features & Deadlines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dissertation Research Grants (DRG)** | Ph.D. Candidates (ABD stage at U.S. institutions) | **$10,000 – $15,000** | Supports direct research costs (data acquisition, surveys, software). Annual deadline in **February**. |
| **Core Research Grants (Presidential)** | PhD Holders / Assistant, Associate & Full Professors | Up to **$75,000** | Fast-track small research grants; 3 cycles/year (LOIs due in March, July, Oct). |
| **Core Research Grants (Trustee)** | PhD Holders / Research Teams | Up to **$200,000** | Major empirical research projects (includes 15% overhead); 3 cycles/year. |
| **Sheldon Danziger Pipeline Grants** | Assistant Professors & Early-Career Faculty | Up to **$65,000** | For early-career faculty (paired with senior mentors); Annual deadline in **October**. |
| **Criminal Justice Grants** | Assistant Professors (Causal designs) | Up to **$100,000** | Co-funded with Arnold Ventures for causal studies on policing, courts, and corrections; Deadline in **April**. |
| **Margaret Olivia Sage (MOS) Grants** | Interdisciplinary Research Teams | Varies (Major) | Team-based research on grand systemic challenges (Democracy, AI, Infrastructure); LOIs in **December**. |
| **Visiting Scholars Program** | Post-PhD / Mid-Career & Senior Scholars | Salary + NYC Housing | Residential fellowship at RSF headquarters in NYC for 1 academic year; Deadline in **June**. |

### Application Mechanics
* **Two-Stage Process (Core Grants):** Requires a 4-page Letter of Inquiry (LOI); invited full proposals are submitted subsequently.
* **Direct Application:** Dissertation (DRG) and Pipeline grants bypass the LOI stage and submit a full proposal directly in the RSF Fluxx portal.
